storyEn: 'Among the club''s recent projects: in April 2019, under president Lorenzo Basola, a partnership with Rise Against Hunger produced 30,000 long-life, nutritious food rations, giving over 100 children a year of schooling safe from exploitation. In the 2019/2020 Rotary year, under president Paolo Pavesi, a contribution to Scuola Media Statale Campi funded a new teaching kitchen focused on inclusion. Together with Rotary Club Cremona Po, the club also furnished a facility in Tidolo serving adults with intellectual disabilities and autism, and since 2014 it has contributed every year, alongside Rotary Club Cremona, to the wheelchair tennis tournament ‘Città di Cremona’.'
